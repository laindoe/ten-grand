Every wave of automation comes with the same two overreactions: it changes nothing, or it changes everything. The truth, as usual, sits in between.

This transmission opens a recurring series examining what AI actually shifts for independent creators — not the headline capabilities, but the day-to-day leverage: what gets faster, what gets cheaper, and what still requires a person who cares.

## What doesn't change

Taste doesn't automate. Neither does trust, or the judgment call to walk away from a deal that looks good on paper. Those stay human, and they stay the hardest part of the job.
