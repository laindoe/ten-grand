Once a season, we ask ten people building inside independent culture the same question and let the ten answers sit side by side, unedited toward consensus.

This installment gathers perspectives from a merchandise designer, a touring manager, a game developer, and seven others who don't usually share a stage — on what's actually changing in how culture gets made and sold.

## Why ten, and why now

No single vantage point captures a moving culture. Ten does a better job, especially when none of the ten agree with each other.
